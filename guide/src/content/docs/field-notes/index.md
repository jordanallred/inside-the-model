Start with a question, then follow it into an experiment. These short notes connect practical questions to the measurements and limits behind an explanation.

- [How do you test an attention-head explanation?](/field-notes/test-an-explanation/) — turn an attractive interpretation into a controlled test.
- [Does lower temperature mean more knowledge?](/field-notes/temperature-and-knowledge/) — distinguish a sharper distribution from a better model.
- [Does GPT-2 read the future?](/replications/gpt2-causality/) — inspect an executed, pinned-model replication and reproduce it on CPU.

New material and substantive corrections appear in [What's new](/changelog/) and its [RSS feed](/rss.xml). The [methods page](/methods/) explains verification and review status; the [teaching guide](/teach/) turns the investigations into a workshop.
